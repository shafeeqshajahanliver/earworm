Earworm

What 650 hits say about how pop is built. Billboard's year-end top 10, every year from 1959 to 2023.

Six decades, one chart

Hits now last a year, often enter the top 10 in their first week, and take a team of four or five to write. The tempo has not moved since 1959.

Sources and coverage

Every number comes from a named source and carries a confidence level. Here is how much of each layer we hold.

Read the findings with these in mind

  • The corpus is America's year-end top 10, so it measures what American radio, sales and later streams rewarded. 81% of the acts are North American.
  • Weeks on chart and debut positions change partly because Billboard changed its rules: sales and airplay tracking from 1991, streaming from the 2010s, and recurrent rules that remove older songs.
  • Tempo and loudness come from 30-second previews. Half-speed readings were corrected with Deezer's own BPM.
  • Song structure comes from expert annotation sets (McGill Billboard, Harmonix, Rolling Stone corpus, Isophonics) plus fan chord sheets (Chordonomicon). Coverage is uneven across decades, so the app shows structure per song but does not chart bridge trends yet.
  • Cover colour is measured on the album artwork, or the current digital artwork where the album has none.
